Exercise 6 · Interactive visualisations

TV energy charts

These charts use the January 2026 TV dataset (Ex6_TVdata_withStar.csv), which lists the labelled energy consumption, star rating, screen size and screen technology of nearly four thousand TV models sold in Australia. Use the buttons to filter the histogram, and hover over the scatterplot to see details for each model.

6.1 / 6.2

How much energy do TVs use?

This histogram shows how many TV models fall into each energy consumption band (kWh per year). Filter by screen technology to compare LED, LCD and OLED models.

6.3 / 6.4

Energy consumption vs star rating

Each circle is one TV model. Colour shows the screen technology. Hover over a circle to see its screen size, technology and brand.

Data: Australian Government Energy Rating register, TV dataset (Jan 2026) supplied for COS30045.
