Visualising the Numbers

Three D3.js charts behind the claims made elsewhere on this site: what screen technology draws the most power, how electricity prices have moved over nearly three decades, and how TV screen sizes are distributed in the dataset this site is built on.

01

Energy consumption by screen technology (55" TVs)

Comparing screen types only makes sense at a fixed screen size, since consumption rises with size. Filtered to 55" models — the median size in the dataset — LED is the highest average draw, narrowly ahead of OLED, with LCD lowest.

02

Electricity spot price, 1998–2024

The average wholesale electricity spot price has trended upward since 1998, with a sharp jump from 2021 onward. This is the backdrop for the "price per kWh" figure used in the calculator on the Televisions page.

03

TV screen size distribution

Most TV models in the dataset fall into the medium size band, with large screens the next most common and small screens the minority — context for why 55" was chosen as the comparison point above.
